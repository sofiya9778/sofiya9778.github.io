hi, i'm Sofiya! i'm a senior at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy. thanks for visiting my home base; i've spent quite a few hours building it :)

a little about me. i have a family of four: myself, my older brother Varshal, my dad, and my mom. i can read, write, and speak gujarati, can speak and understand hindi, and know the basics of mandarin. i love my friends a lot, almost as much as i love taco bell. if you know me, you know i'm spending my free time building and playing on roblox, playing fortnite, pickleball, badminton, making new foods, painting, scrolling on pinterest, or napping. my favorite color is yellow!

since i was little, i have naturally been drawn to puzzles. as i grew older, i realized coding is the most complex a puzzle can get, and i started spending time trying to fit together the pieces. however, when i walked into my first stem class, PLTW, i was floored. i was the only girl out of the 32 students. that's where my story began.
